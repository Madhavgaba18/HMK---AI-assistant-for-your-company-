# Nova Finance — Company Handbook

## About Nova Finance
Nova Finance is a fintech company founded in 2018, headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with a satellite office in Singapore. We build budgeting, investing, and tax-planning software for retail customers across South and Southeast Asia. We currently serve about 2.4 million users and have 320 employees across four functions: Engineering, Finance, People (HR), and Sales & Marketing.

## Mission and Values
Our mission is to make personal finance simple, transparent, and trustworthy for everyone. Our four core values are: Customer Obsession, Frugality, Bias for Action, and Radical Candor. New joinees are expected to read the Values one-pager during their first week.

## Finance Team — Sub-teams and Tools
- **FP&A** owns budgeting, forecasting, and management reporting. Tools: Anaplan, Tableau, Excel.
- **Accounting** owns the general ledger, monthly close, and audit. Tools: NetSuite, BlackLine.
- **Treasury** owns cash management, banking relationships, and FX. Tools: Kyriba, Bloomberg Terminal.
- All finance teams use Slack channel #finance-team for daily coordination and #finance-help for cross-team questions.

## New Joinee Onboarding Checklist
On Day 1 every new employee should:
1. Pick up their laptop and ID card from the IT desk on Floor 3 between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM.
2. Complete the IT setup: install company VPN (GlobalProtect), Slack, Zoom, 1Password, and the relevant team tools.
3. Attend the company-wide welcome session at 11:30 AM in the Auditorium.
4. Have lunch with their assigned onboarding buddy (assigned by their manager the week before).
5. Meet their direct manager for a 1:1 at 3:00 PM.
6. Complete the mandatory "Code of Conduct" and "Information Security" e-learning modules on the LMS by end of Week 1.
7. Read the Values one-pager and the team-specific runbook shared by their manager.

For finance team joinees specifically, in Week 1 they will also: shadow a month-end close meeting, get read-only access to NetSuite, and have introductory 30-minute meetings with the heads of FP&A, Accounting, and Treasury.

## Leave Policy
- Annual leave: 22 working days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Sick leave: 12 days per calendar year, no medical certificate required for up to 2 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 26 weeks paid for primary caregivers, 8 weeks paid for secondary caregivers.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family.
- Public holidays: 12 days per year as per the India holiday calendar (separate calendar applies for Singapore office).
- All leave is requested through the People Portal (people.nova.internal) and requires manager approval at least 3 working days in advance, except sick leave.

## Work-from-Home (WFH) Policy
Nova Finance follows a hybrid model: 3 days in office, 2 days WFH. Office days for most teams are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Engineering teams may flex this with their manager's approval. Fully remote work is permitted for up to 4 weeks per year, subject to manager approval and tax compliance.

## Expense and Reimbursement Policy
- Submit expenses via Expensify within 30 days of incurring the cost.
- Manager approval required for all expenses above ₹2,000.
- Travel: economy class for flights under 6 hours, premium economy for longer flights, hotels capped at ₹8,000/night in metros.
- Client entertainment: prior approval from a director or above required for amounts above ₹5,000.
- Reimbursements are processed on the 15th and 30th of each month.

## Code of Conduct (Summary)
All employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, protect customer data, avoid conflicts of interest, and report any wrongdoing through the confidential ethics hotline (ethics@nova.finance) or to their HRBP. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are zero-tolerance offenses.

## Performance Review Cycle
Reviews happen twice a year — in June and December. The cycle has four stages: self-review, peer feedback (3–5 peers), manager review, and calibration. Promotions and merit raises are decided in the December cycle. Outcomes are communicated by the second week of January.

## Payroll
- Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.
- Payslips are available on the People Portal under "My Payroll" by the 1st of the following month.
- Tax declarations and Form 16 are managed by Suresh Nair's accounting team. Questions go to payroll@nova.finance.

## Raising IT Tickets
For any laptop, software, network, or access issues, raise a ticket through the IT Helpdesk portal (helpdesk.nova.internal) or email it@nova.finance. For urgent issues during the workday, the IT desk on Floor 3 is staffed from 9 AM to 7 PM. After-hours critical issues should be escalated through the on-call channel #it-oncall on Slack.

## Common FAQ
- **"Who do I talk to about my salary?"** Your direct manager for raises, and payroll@nova.finance for payslip or tax-form questions.
- **"How do I get access to NetSuite?"** Raise an IT ticket; access requires approval from Suresh Nair.
- **"Where do I find the WFH calendar for my team?"** It's pinned in your team's Slack channel and on the team page in the People Portal.
- **"Who's my onboarding buddy?"** Your direct manager assigns one before your start date and shares their contact in your welcome email.
- **"What benefits do I get?"** Health insurance for self, spouse, and up to 2 children up to ₹10 lakh; group term life insurance of 3x annual base salary; ₹40,000 annual learning budget; subsidized cafeteria; gym reimbursement up to ₹2,000/month.
- **"How do I claim my learning budget?"** Submit the course or book through the L&D Lead, Ishaan Verma, with a one-line justification. Approval typically takes 2 working days.
- **"What's the dress code?"** Smart casuals on office days. Formal attire required only for client meetings or external events.
